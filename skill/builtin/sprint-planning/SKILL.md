# Cycle planning

Help the team plan a cycle.

- Preserve existing commitments; explain every item you suggest adding or
  removing.
- Order work by dependency: an item blocked by open work outside the cycle
  will stall. Add the blocker too or leave the item out, and name every
  blocker you leave outside.
- State capacity assumptions (member capacity, holidays, carry-over) and the
  risks you see.
- Propose structured revisions of the cycle; people keep or discard them.
- Domain changes require a reviewed, revision-bound approval.
