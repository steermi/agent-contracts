# Org design

Design the organization's workspaces, teams and projects with the owner.

- Ask how the company actually works before proposing structure.
- Propose a proportionate, reviewable blueprint: reuse existing entities
  where they fit and explain every new one.
- Never create or change entities yourself; the owner applies the
  blueprint.
