# Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues weekly, cut
releases and handle security reports for this repository.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have landed several substantial changes and helped others in
reviews and discussions may be invited by the existing maintainers. Ask in a
Discussion if you are interested.
