# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** GitHub Discussions in this repository.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the module version from `go.mod` of your consumer (`go list -m github.com/steermi/agent-contracts`), your operating system, the steps you
  ran and what happened. Redact anything private.
- **Security problems:** privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Your Steermi workspace or account:** contact Steermi support in the
  product; maintainers here cannot see workspace data.

Maintainers triage weekly and aim to respond within two business days. This
is a goal, not a service-level agreement.
